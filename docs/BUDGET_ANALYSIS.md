## Loan Options Ranked

### 🥇 USDA Direct 502 Loan — BEST OPTION

**Why it's best for you:**
- 0% down payment
- Payment assistance subsidizes your interest rate (effective rate as low as 1%)
- You pay only 24% of your adjusted income toward housing
- Coos County income limit: $143,450 — **you qualify**
- Covers rural areas with 1+ acre

**How payment assistance works:**
USDA calculates your "adjusted income" (gross minus deductions for household size, elderly, disabled). Your monthly payment = the LESSER of:
1. 24% of adjusted annual income ÷ 12
2. P&I calculated at 1% interest

The government pays the difference between your subsidized payment and the full 5.25% payment.

### 🥈 USDA Guaranteed Loan

- 0% down, but no interest subsidy (you pay full rate ~6.5%)
- Guarantee fee: 0.35%/yr added to payment
- Higher payment than Direct, but easier qualification

### 🥉 FHA Loan (3.5% down)

- Needs $6,125 down on $175k — could come from 401k IRA withdrawal
- PMI required (0.55%/yr)
- Higher total payment than USDA options

### Conventional (10–20% down)

- Needs $17,500–$40,000 down — significant 401k hit
- PMI if under 20%
- Highest payment of all options
